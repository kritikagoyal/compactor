## Summary
Compare Amazon RDS Proxy and PgBouncer for PostgreSQL connection pooling on AWS. This post explains how each pooler handles pooling modes, authentication, failover, and session-state pinning, and gives you a framework to choose between a fully managed service and a self-managed pooler.
