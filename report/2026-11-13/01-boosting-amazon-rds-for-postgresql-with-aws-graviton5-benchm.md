## Summary
AWS Graviton5 is now available on Amazon RDS through the new M9g instance family. In this post, we benchmark M9g against the M6g, M7g, and M8g generations for Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL, measuring throughput, latency, and price-performance so you can decide whether an upgrade is worth it.
