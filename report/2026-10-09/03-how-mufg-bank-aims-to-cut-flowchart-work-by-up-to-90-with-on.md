## Summary
MUFG Bank standardized overseas back-office procedures across roughly 30 countries by grounding generative AI in a structured domain model on Amazon Neptune and Amazon Bedrock. Its AI Flowchart application is expected to cut the system-development effort behind each branch-procedure standardization from 11 person-days to 1, a reduction of up to 90%.
