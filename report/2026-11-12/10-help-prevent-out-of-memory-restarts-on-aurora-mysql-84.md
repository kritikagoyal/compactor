## Summary
Out-of-memory restarts drop every connection and force an Aurora MySQL cluster restart. Learn how Aurora MySQL 8.4's automatic memory management prevents them: confirm protection is active, read the signals during a memory-pressure event, monitor OOM avoidance with CloudWatch, and decide when to take manual control.
