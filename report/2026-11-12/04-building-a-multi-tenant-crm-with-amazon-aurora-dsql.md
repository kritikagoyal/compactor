## Summary
Learn how to design and implement a multi-tenant CRM on Amazon Aurora DSQL using the pooled tenancy model. Tenant isolation is enforced in a centralized data-access layer, with Aurora DSQL-specific design considerations, and a working proof of concept you can deploy on Amazon ECS Express Mode.
