## Summary
Choosing a multi-tenancy isolation model for Amazon ElastiCache for Valkey comes up in nearly every SaaS architecture review. This post walks through the options, from dedicated clusters to shared infrastructure with per-tenant encryption, and shows how to match each model to your blast radius and compliance requirements.
