## Summary
In Part 2 of this series, you connect your Kiro DBA assistant to your team's runbooks and policies. Store runbooks in Amazon S3, index them with Amazon Bedrock Knowledge Bases on Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL using pgvector, and wire up retrieval so the assistant grounds its recommendations in your own documentation.
