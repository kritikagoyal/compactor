## Summary
DynamoDB caching with Amazon ElastiCache Serverless for Valkey brings write-through caching to Amazon DynamoDB with sub-millisecond reads and minimal application changes. Serve hot keys from cache, reduce read throttling, and lower read costs while your application keeps calling the DynamoDB API as it does today.
