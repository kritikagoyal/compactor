## Summary
U.S. Bank migrated 119 TB across four Oracle Exadata databases to Amazon RDS for Oracle over a bandwidth-limited AWS Direct Connect link. This post walks through the automated migration pipeline they built with Oracle Transportable Tablespaces (XTTS), AWS DataSync, and Amazon EFS, completing the initial baseline copy in 7 days with zero data loss.
