## Summary
Bulk put items is a fully managed feature that loads data from Amazon S3 directly into your existing Amazon DynamoDB tables, with no impact to your table's read and write performance and at a lower price point than per-request writes. This post introduces the feature, walks through running an import end to end, and shares best practices for production.
