## Summary
Database selection is a foundational decision when building an application. Choosing the right database enables you to focus effort on building customer value while the database scales with demand. AWS offers several fully managed database services. That breadth serves the diversity of the millions of customers who operate databases on AWS, but it raises a practical question: Where do I start? Based on our collective experience working with database customers, this post provides a direct, opinionated answer focused on the job you are trying to perform: migrating an existing workload, refactoring a legacy application, or building something new.
