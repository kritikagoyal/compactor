## Summary
Today, we are announcing the general availability of distributed KV caching with Amazon ElastiCache for Valkey, a capability that cuts self-hosted LLM inference costs by up to 40% and time to first token by up to 80%. Learn how it works, review benchmark results across workloads and models, deploy it on your own GPU fleet, and size the savings with the open-source KVTrace tool.
